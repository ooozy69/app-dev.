# My Favorite Movie

## Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse

**Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse** is one of my favorite animated movies. I like this movie because of its unique animation, interesting story, and inspiring characters.

### About the Movie

The movie follows Miles Morales, a teenager who becomes Spider-Man and meets different versions of Spider-Man from other dimensions. Together, they work to save their worlds.

### My Favorite Characters

1. Miles Morales
2. Peter B. Parker
3. Gwen Stacy
4. Spider-Man Noir
5. Spider-Ham

### Why I Like This Movie

- The animation is creative and colorful.
- The story is exciting and meaningful.
- The characters have different personalities.
- It teaches us to believe in ourselves.

### My Favorite Quote
